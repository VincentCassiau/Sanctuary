# Changelog

## 1.2.0

**Sanctuary speaks your language**

- Sanctuary is now available in German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Italian, as well as English and French. These five translations are drafts that no native speaker has reviewed yet: if a word sounds wrong, corrections are welcome on GitHub.
- New Language setting in the Advanced tab. Sanctuary follows your game's language by default, or you can pick another one for Sanctuary alone: the game keeps its own.
- Russian text is readable on any client, and the window makes room for longer translations.

**Fixes**

- Thin borders and separator lines that could disappear at some UI scales are drawn again.
- The header tooltip always starts with a capital letter.
- A few labels no longer overlap or get cut off when the window is narrow.

## 1.1.0

- Mail from a filtered person can be deleted when you open the mailbox, with settings for attachments and the minimap icon. Mail that did not come from a player is never touched.
- /say and /yell are one option, and the public channel modes are one menu.
- What's new is announced in chat for a day after an update.
- The window follows your screen height.
- The chat line says in words what was blocked, and the Journal shows Name-Realm everywhere.

## 1.0.0

- First public release: only the people you know can contact you, blocked names and patterns, anti-spam for the public channels, and the Journal.
